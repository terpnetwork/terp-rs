# Headstash 2026: team brief

We're proposing two Terp governance proposals that launch Headstash 2026, a private TERP airdrop to holders from 15 EVM NFT communities plus the Solana community stoned-ape-club. 17,791 eligible identities share 66,456,669 TERP from the community pool, split into 98,841 notes. Proposal 1/2 stores the zero-knowledge note circuit. Proposal 2/2 creates and funds the airdrop contract and an on-chain, hash-verified claim page. To claim, holders first register by signing a message with the wallet that held the NFT. They then claim with a zero-knowledge proof, so their Terp address isn't linked on-chain to that wallet. Claim fees are covered by fee grants funded by the foundation DAO, so claimers need no tokens. Governance (x/gov) is the admin of both contracts, and WAVS is disabled. Claims close at block 44,444,444, and anything left unclaimed is burned. Penumbra-based privacy after the claim is planned but not live yet.

Order and signers: terp1gnfq (the dabarber validator key) proposes using Zigner together with a short-lived session key. Each proposal needs a 10,000 TERP deposit (5,000 at submission plus a 5,000 top-up). That wallet can only fund one proposal at a time, so the proposals run one after the other: 1/2 first, then 2/2 after 1/2 passes and its deposit is refunded (or a second depositor adds the top-up). After 2/2 passes, terp1fx issues the claimer fee grants and the small capped grant for the claim-page upload, then revokes the upload grant. terp1fx first needs a top-up of about 2,572 THIOL. Still pending before anything is signed: (1) host the two metadata files in `tools/headstash/deploy/proposal-metadata/` (e.g. on IPFS), confirm the hosted copies match `SHA256SUMS`, and fill in their URIs; (2) regenerate the session grant at signing time; (3) check on the Zigner device that each sign document renders correctly. The step-by-step checklist is `tools/headstash/deploy/OPERATOR-CHECKLIST.md`. The discussion is at https://discord.terp.network.
